Industry based solutions

Automotive

Automotive lines require variant handling, cycle-time discipline and traceability at the same time. Akrosis builds assembly verification, fixture-based measurement and surface defect inspection together with PLC, robot and operator interfaces.

Explore solutions

Part presence, clip and fastener verification, orientation checks, dimensional measurement and surface defect inspection are the most common needs.

The decision chain is tied directly to PLC and robot flow, using multi-trigger logic, pre-filtering and recipe-based operation to keep timing under control.

Recipes can be selected automatically from model or station information so that the same cell can run different variants safely.

Inspection result, reference image and process data can be stored against product or serial information and transferred to SQL or upper systems.

3D sensing is used for flatness checks, dent/depression detection, form difference analysis and robot-guided reference tasks.

Camera, lighting, mechanical support, PLC, safety logic and operator interface are commissioned as one coordinated scope.
