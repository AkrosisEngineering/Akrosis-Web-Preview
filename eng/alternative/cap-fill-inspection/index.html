Cap, fill and label inspection

The challenge

Inconsistent cap quality, fill level variation and label alignment issues were still dependent on operator checks.

Our solution

A multi-camera vision system, dedicated lighting, recipe-based decision logic and rejection mechanism were connected in one cycle.

The impact

Inspection became repeatable and the quality team started tracking defects through measurable, reportable data.
