We shared the Akrosis journey at the TeknoPar'24 conference

June 16, 20263 min read

Invited by Karabuk University IEEE, we joined TeknoPar'24 to share our founding story, project experience and engineering perspective with students.

ConferenceIEEEUniversity event

Highlights

  • Founding story and field experience were shared
  • Real project examples were discussed with students
  • Entrepreneurship and engineering approach were presented

Akrosis Engineering joined the TeknoPar'24 Conference with the invitation of Karabuk University IEEE.

During the event, we shared our company’s founding story together with the experience, decisions and engineering path that started during student years and extended into field applications.

Using projects developed with our partner Fomsan Makine as examples, we explained how we approach real production problems and which engineering principles guide our solution work.

This conversation with future engineers and entrepreneurs was a valuable experience for us as well. We thank the IEEE Karabuk team for their hospitality.
