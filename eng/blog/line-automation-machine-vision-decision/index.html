The role of machine vision decisions in line automation projects

June 14, 20264 min read

A project note on connecting OK/NOK decisions to rejection mechanisms, operator screens, alarms and reporting flows.

Line automationPLCReporting

Highlights

  • The decision drives the automation scenario
  • Reject and alarm flow are managed in one chain
  • HMI feedback helps maintain operator speed

A machine-vision decision is not only an OK/NOK output; it also drives rejection hardware, operator messaging and alarm behavior. That is why the decision layer must sit inside the automation architecture itself.

A proper integration keeps the visual decision synchronized with robot, conveyor and HMI behavior so the inspection point improves process safety without slowing production.
